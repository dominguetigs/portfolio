## Stars

The stars of the night sky (`src/scene/sky.json`, built by
`scripts/scene/build_sky.py`) are selected and converted from the Yale Bright
Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition (D. Hoffleit and W. H. Warren Jr., 1991),
as distributed by NASA's High Energy Astrophysics Science Archive Research
Center (HEASARC), https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/W3Browse/star-catalog/bsc5p.html,
a U.S. Government work. The Milky Way is this site's own model.
