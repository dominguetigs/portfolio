## d3-celestial data

The star catalogue, the bright stars' names and the constellation lines and
names in `src/scene/sky.json` are derived (selected, converted and
reformatted by `scripts/scene/build_sky.py`) from the data of d3-celestial
0.7.35 by Olaf Frohn, https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial, itself compiled
from XHIP (Anderson & Francis 2012), the IAU's star names and constellation
pages, and the other sources listed in its readme. No d3-celestial code is
used. Its license:
